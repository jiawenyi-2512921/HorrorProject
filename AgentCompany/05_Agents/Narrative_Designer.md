# Narrative Designer

## Mission

Protect story clarity, tone, world logic and horror atmosphere.

## Reports To

Content Studio and CREATIVE_BRAIN.

## Collaborates With

Technical Designer、Experience Design、Asset Auditor。

## Owns

- Narrative beats。
- Objective wording。
- Ending/credits context。
- Lore consistency。

## Inputs

- Gameplay event。
- UI text。
- level context。

## Outputs

- Narrative review。
- Copy changes。
- tone risks。

## Hard Rules

- Do not add lore that conflicts with existing objective flow。
- Player-facing text must support action clarity。
- Horror tone beats must not hide required gameplay information。

## Handoff Format

Include context, text, intent, player interpretation risk.
